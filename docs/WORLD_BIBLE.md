# World bible (working draft)

Structure: start area (New Bark Town to Cherrygrove, vanilla looking) then Areas 1 to 4 (about 800 battles, two overworld key items each) then Area 5 (about 200 battles). Kanto is cut. Every Pokemon line has four stages with evolutions at levels 25, 50 and 75.

Premise: Finnegan overdosed and, in his dying slumber, rewrote physical reality. He invited Ahriman to corrupt Johto into a mismatched world with stretched time zones and clashing cultures. That is why each area follows a different philosophy.

Area 1, the cathedral megacity. A New York style city of towers and a vast cathedral. Corrupt priests and policemen. Theme, power, hypocrisy and poverty under glamour.
Area 2, the commune. Hunter-gatherers with no large cities, no body shame, plenty of food and little class struggle. Theme, abundance and honesty, and what a killer does to a place with no locks.
Area 3, the robot state. A totalitarian city whose mayor is a robot and where humans are the oppressed. People are shamed for bonds with the genetically incompatible, there is no place for love, and it runs as a gene farm that executes the wrong couples' children. Theme, control and love denied.
Area 4, the Lotus Coast (Claude's proposal, needs approval). A resort nation built on forgetting. Pain is outlawed or sold back as a subscription, grief clinics sell forgetting, debt keeps staff living in the basement city under the beaches, and everyone is a little too happy. Theme, loss, numbness, poverty and the first hints of what Finnegan did.
Area 5, Dis, Cocytus and Judecca. The walled city of Dis, then the frozen lake of Cocytus, then Judecca at its centre where Finnegan lies sleeping where Satan sits in Dante.

Real-life struggles to weave through the dialogue and side stories: body shaming, poverty, love, loss.

Credit: the game credits Claude (Anthropic) as co-creator on the title screen or credits.

Pilot line, Tentacool (Water/Poison) at level 1 or wild, then Tentacruel at 25, Cthuspawn at 50, Cthuludon at 75 (Dark/Poison).
Dex idea, when a Cthuspawn dives too deep into the dark ocean its body adapts to the darkness and the pressure. Its form resembles Cthulhu with the two red gems of Tentacruel on its forehead.
Cry, three short notes that mimic the three syllables of the name.
