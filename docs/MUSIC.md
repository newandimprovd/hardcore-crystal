# Original music

Eight ambient songs composed by rules (`tools/hardcore/gen_music.py`, seeded, deterministic). Three channels, long loops, lots of silence. Every phrase is a transformation of a small motif, so no bar repeats within a loop.

Moods:

- **The Road**: wind over a road nobody maintains.
- **The Tithe**: a church bell tolling over an accounting office.
- **The Grove**: warm, a little too warm, a drone that hums along.
- **The Axiom**: a machine that counts in sevens while the bass counts in fives.
- **The Lotus**: a lounge singer who left a long time ago.
- **The City of Dis**: a low furnace and a semitone that will not resolve.
- **The Terraces**: every phrase climbs one step higher than the last, then the section starts again at the bottom.
- **The Abyss**: almost silence, and something very large breathing in it.
